# Origin

This repo exists to show that Kinetic Gain's control-plane pattern extends cleanly into biotech and diagnostics clinical-event review.

The primitive is not generic "case review." The real workflow pressure lives in:

- narrative chronology that still needs event-timing clarification
- seriousness framing that must align with the narrative packet
- follow-up and physician addenda that determine whether the board can trust the case
- literature and submission posture that determine whether the packet is outbound-safe

`clinical-event-narrative-review-board` packages that pressure into a buyer-readable and operator-readable public surface.
